Egyptian Collections in Japan

From Ancient Egypt to Coptic Textiles

🗼

Tokyo & Kanto Region

Ancient Orient Museum

古代オリエント博物館 (Ikebukuro)

Must Visit: Comprehensive collection of Ancient Near East and Egypt.
Visit Website

Tokyo National Museum

東京国立博物館 (Ueno)

Highlights: Mummies and artifacts in the Toyokan (Asian Gallery).
Visit Website

Tokai University Museum

東海大学文明研究所 (Kanagawa)

Highlights: Significant collection of papyri (A.H. Gardiner collection).

Ancient Egyptian Museum

古代エジプト美術館 (Shibuya)

Specialty: Japan's only museum dedicated exclusively to Ancient Egypt.
Visit Website

Matsuoka Museum of Art

松岡美術館 (Shirokanedai)

Collection: Excellent Ancient Egyptian artifacts and Gandhara Buddhist sculptures.
Visit Website

Teikyo University Museum

帝京大学総合博物館 (Hachioji)

Highlights: Egyptian archaeological collection.
Visit Website

Middle Eastern Culture Center

中近東文化センター (Mitaka)

Specialty: Extensive collection from the Ancient Near East and Egypt.
Visit Website

Artizon Museum

アーティゾン美術館 (Kyobashi)

Highlights: Ancient Egyptian antiquities alongside modern art.
Visit Website

Intermediatheque (IMT)

インターメディアテク (Marunouchi)

Highlights: University of Tokyo collection including Egyptian mummies.
Visit Website
🏯

Chubu & Kansai Region

MOA Museum of Art

MOA美術館 (Atami, Shizuoka)

Highlights: Scenic museum with Egyptian antiquities.
Visit Website

Northern Culture Museum

北方文化博物館 (Niigata)

Highlights: Dedicated archaeology hall with Egyptian artifacts.
Visit Website

MIHO MUSEUM

ミホ・ミュージアム (Shiga)

Highlights: World-class Egyptian collection in a stunning I.M. Pei building.
Visit Website

Tenri University Sankokan

天理大学附属天理参考館 (Nara)

Highlights: Extensive ethnographic and archaeological collection including Egypt.
Visit Website
⛩️

Chugoku Region

Ohara Museum of Art

大原美術館 (Kurashiki, Okayama)

Highlights: Famous for Western art but houses Egyptian antiquities collected by Kojima Torajiro.
Visit Website

Kibi International University Museum

吉備国際大学考古学資料館 (Takahashi, Okayama)

Highlights: Archaeological collection including Near Eastern and Egyptian materials.

Okayama Orient Museum

岡山市立オリエント美術館 (Okayama)

Highlights: Specialized in Ancient Near East and Egyptian art.
Visit Website

Shimonoseki City Art Museum

下関市立美術館 (Yamaguchi)

Highlights: Historic Egyptian collection.
🧶

Coptic Textile Collections in Japan

Japan houses several exquisite collections of Coptic textiles.

Toyama Memorial Museum of Art

遠山記念館 (Saitama)

Specialty: Renowned collection of Coptic textiles and ancient fabrics.
Visit Website

Other Collections

Significant Coptic textiles can also be found at:

  • Ancient Orient Museum (Tokyo)
  • Okayama Orient Museum (Okayama)
  • Tenri University Sankokan Museum (Nara)
  • Kanegafuchi Foundation (Collection often exhibited at major museums)

These institutions rotate their textile displays for conservation purposes. Please check current exhibitions before visiting.
