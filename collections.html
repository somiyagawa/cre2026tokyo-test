Egyptian Collections in Japan

From Ancient Egypt to Coptic Textiles

🗼

Tokyo & Kanto Region

Ancient Orient Museum

古代オリエント博物館 (Ikebukuro)

Highlights: Funerary stela for Padikhonsu (Ptolemaic) and Ancient Near Eastern artifacts.
Visit Website

Tokyo National Museum

東京国立博物館 (Ueno)

Highlights: Cartonnage coffin & mummy of Pasherienptah (22nd Dyn), Sekhmet statue (Amenhotep III).
Visit Website

Tokai University Museum

東海大学文明研究所 (Kanagawa)

Highlights: Hachishi Suzuki collection including Demotic and Hieratic papyri.

Ancient Egyptian Museum

古代エジプト美術館 (Shibuya)

Specialty: Tadashi Kikugawa collection. Japan's only museum dedicated exclusively to Ancient Egypt.
Visit Website

Matsuoka Museum of Art

松岡美術館 (Shirokanedai)

Collection: Coffin of a priest of Min from Akhmim (ca. 4th c. BCE), Sekhmet statue, and polychrome reliefs.
Visit Website

Middle Eastern Culture Center

中近東文化センター (Mitaka)

Specialty: Includes relief of Thutmose III and a wooden ship model.
Visit Website

Artizon Museum

アーティゾン美術館 (Kyobashi)

Highlights: Ancient Egyptian antiquities (including Sekhmet statue) alongside modern art.
Visit Website

Intermediatheque (IMT)

インターメディアテク (Marunouchi)

Highlights: Triple-nested coffin and mummy (donated 1888) from University of Tokyo collection.
Visit Website
🏯

Chubu & Kansai Region

The Kyoto University Museum

京都大学総合博物館 (Kyoto)

Highlights: ~1500 items donated by Flinders Petrie to Kosaku Hamada. Excavated artifacts from Naucratis, Thebes, etc.
Visit Website

MIHO MUSEUM

ミホ・ミュージアム (Shiga)

Highlights: Silver Falcon God statue, Ptolemaic queen statue (Arsinoe II), and glass king head.
Visit Website

Tenri University Sankokan

天理大学附属天理参考館 (Nara)

Highlights: Wooden anthropoid coffin from the Ptolemaic period and New Kingdom stelae.
Visit Website

Northern Culture Museum

北方文化博物館 (Niigata)

Highlights: Dedicated archaeology hall with Egyptian artifacts.
Visit Website
⛩️

Chugoku Region

Ohara Museum of Art

大原美術館 (Kurashiki, Okayama)

Highlights: Collection of Torajiro Kojima including a large faience vessel of Sheshonq I (22nd Dyn).
Visit Website

Nariwa Museum

高梁市成羽美術館 (Takahashi, Okayama)

Highlights: Torajiro Kojima collection including a shabti of Pinedjem II from the Royal Cache (DB320).
Visit Website

Okayama Orient Museum

岡山市立オリエント美術館 (Okayama)

Highlights: Shinjiro Yasuhara donation. Includes shabtis from the Royal Cache (Neskhons, Isetemkheb).
Visit Website

Shimonoseki City Art Museum

下関市立美術館 (Yamaguchi)

Highlights: Kinpei Takeuchi collection gathered in London during the late Meiji period.
🧶

Coptic Textile Collections in Japan

Japan houses several exquisite collections of Coptic textiles.

Toyama Memorial Museum of Art

遠山記念館 (Saitama)

Specialty: Old Kingdom king statue, New Kingdom stela, and Coptic textiles.
Visit Website

Other Collections

Significant Coptic textiles can also be found at:

  • Ancient Orient Museum (Tokyo)
  • Okayama Orient Museum (Okayama)
  • Tenri University Sankokan Museum (Nara)
  • Kanegafuchi Foundation (Collection often exhibited at major museums)

These institutions rotate their textile displays for conservation purposes. Please check current exhibitions before visiting.
