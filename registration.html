Registration Fees

Category Fee
Early Registration JPY 8,000
Late Registration JPY 10,000

Payment Information

Registration fees must be paid by bank transfer or Wise. All payments must be made in Japanese yen (¥, JPY).

Registration Fee Covers

  • Attendance at all keynote lectures and conference sessions
  • Opening drinks reception
  • Conference welcome pack
  • Museum tour (train ticket not included)

Note: The conference dinner and excursion are not included in the registration fee.

Conference Dinner

Date & Time: Monday, 13 July, 19:00-21:00
Venue: MY Shokudo Dining & Sakaba, near Tokyo Station
Participation Fee: JPY 10,000
Status: Optional, can be booked separately at registration time

Museum Visit

Venue: Ancient Orient Museum in Ikebukuro
Date: Sunday, 12 July
Cost: Included in registration fee (excluding transportation between venue and museum)
