Registration

Registration Fees & Information

Status: Registration for CRE 2026 is now open. Early registration has closed; late registration is open until 1 July 2026.

Register for CRE 26 Tokyo

Registration has two steps: (1) pay the appropriate fee via Wise or bank transfer, then (2) complete the official registration form below. You will be asked for your personal details, affiliation, presentation information (if applicable), and your choice of optional events (conference dinner and excursion).

Open the Registration Form

Please complete the form only after you have made your payment. The form opens in a new tab via Google Forms.

Download Second Circular (PDF)

Registration Fees

Category Fee
Early Registration (Closed) JPY 8,000
Late Registration (until 1 July 2026) JPY 10,000
Conference Dinner (optional) JPY 10,000
Excursion — Kamakura & Enoshima (optional) JPY 8,000

Registration fees must be paid by Wise or bank transfer. All payments must be made in Japanese yen (¥, JPY), and any bank or transfer fees must be covered by the sender.

The Registration Fee Covers

  • Attendance at all keynote lectures and conference sessions
  • Opening drinks reception
  • Conference welcome pack
  • Museum tour (train ticket not included)

Note: The conference dinner and excursion are not included in the registration fee.
