Achievement

318

The crowdfunding campaign "Support the First Conference in Japan of the International Egyptological Conference for Early-Career Researchers" (Prof. So Miyagawa's Lab, University of Tsukuba × CRE26 Tokyo Organizing Committee; READYFOR; 23 March – 5 June 2026) was launched to subsidize travel and accommodation for early-career researchers attending CRE 26 Tokyo — especially those from Egypt — as well as excursions and other academic exchange activities, and to enhance the quality of the conference.

Thanks to your generous support, the campaign far exceeded its initial goal of ¥3,000,000 and surpassed its next goal of ¥5,000,000, ultimately raising ¥5,689,000 — approximately 1.9 times the initial goal — with 318 donors in total. We are sincerely grateful for your support.
